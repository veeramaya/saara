Capture

Every task holds more than a checkbox

Set a commitment with everything it needs in one place — so none of it lives only in your head.

  • ✓When & how long. Pick a start, tap a duration — 15m to 2h — and Saara sets the end.
  • ✓Where & who. A venue, and the people involved.
  • ✓Meeting link. Drop a Google Meet or join link right on the task.
  • ✓Repeat. Once, daily, Mon–Fri, weekly or monthly — on the days you choose.
The flagship

Run the event, live

A big commitment isn't one checkbox — it's a sequence you execute. Break an event into timed action items, hit Start agenda, and run it minute by minute while you're in the room.

  • ✓Timed, in sequence. Each step gets its own slot — 2:00–2:15, 2:15–2:30 — so the plan paces you.
  • ✓Start agenda. A live tracker for the event; tick steps off as you perform.
  • ✓From notes. Turn meeting notes into action items in a tap.
  • ✓Still one commitment. The event keeps its people, its Meet link, its area — and its own reliability.

e.g. a 2-hour product demo → 8 action items: Introduce · Set context · Demo Fitcard · Demo virtual try-on …

The moat

Private by design, not by promise

Most apps ask you to trust their servers. Saara doesn't have any. Your practice stays on your machines — encrypted, accountless, and yours.

On-device & encrypted

Your ledger lives in an encrypted database on your own device. No account, nothing to breach.

No ads, no tracking

No advertising ID, no profiling. You are not the product — you're the point.

Yours to take

Export an encrypted snapshot anytime and restore it anywhere. The ledger is its own backup.

Your AI, your key

Bring your own AI — nothing through Realmaya

Ask Saara runs on your key, stored only in this device's secure keystore. Saara talks to the provider directly — your prompts never pass through Realmaya's servers, because there aren't any.

  • ✓Your provider, your model. Anthropic Claude and more — pick the exact model.
  • ✓Stored on-device. In the OS secure keystore, encrypted at rest.
  • ✓Direct to the provider. No middleman, no logging by us.
