Tasks

Every task holds more than a checkbox

A commitment in Saara carries its full context — so none of it lives only in your head. Capture it in a breath; find it in an instant.

  • ✓Time, place & duration. When it’s due, where it happens, how long it takes.
  • ✓People & meeting links. Who’s involved, with the call link right on the task.
  • ✓Area, notes & recurrence. Tag it, note it, repeat it — “every Sunday, 8:00 AM”.
  • ✓Honest status & source. Kept, missed or declined — and where it came from.

Then search across all of it — titles, notes, people, even links — and filter by type, date, area, status or source.

Serverless sync

Desktop and phone, always in step

Saara lives on your Windows desktop and travels with you on your phone. Pair once over your own Wi-Fi and they reconcile themselves — no cloud in the middle, ever. Two laptops and two phones? They all converge, because the history is an append-only ledger that can't conflict.

  • ✓Any number of devices. Your practice spreads as you sync.
