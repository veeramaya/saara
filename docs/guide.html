Help & User Guide

Using Saara

Everything you need to give your word, keep it, and see your reliability grow. Saara is local-first — your data lives encrypted on your own device, and nothing goes through a server.

1Getting started

Saara has five tabs along the bottom:

  • Today — your day, and the integrity circle. Zoom chips: Today / Week / 2 weeks / Month.
  • Tasks — every commitment; search by title, notes, people or links, and filter by Type · Date · Area · Status · Source.
  • Areas — the parts of your life, each with its own score.
  • Progress — your reliability over time, on the ladder.
  • Saara — the assistant. Ask it to find things or do things (needs your AI key — see §10).

Two kinds of commitment

A Task is a to-do (it syncs with Google Tasks). An Event is time-blocked (it syncs two-way with Google Calendar). Both can repeat.

Draft vs. Release — the most important idea

When you add something, the main button says Release, with Save as draft beneath it.

  • Draft = thinking out loud. It travels between your devices, but is not pushed to Google and does not count in your score.
  • Release = giving your word. From then on it syncs everywhere and counts. Editing a released item shows the button as plain Save.
Why it matters: only what happens after you Release affects your reliability. Nothing counts against you while it's a draft.

Tap the + Task button. You'll get a menu of ways to capture:

AI is optional. The deterministic Parse works with no key. The AI options (reading screenshots, structuring pasted text) use your own AI key — see §10. If a field looks uncertain, Saara highlights it: "Some fields look uncertain — please review before saving."

3Tasks & events in depth

A task or event can hold a lot — so nothing lives only in your head:

  • When — date + time. No date? It sits in an Unscheduled section on Today until you give it a time.
  • Duration — chips 15 / 30 / 45 / 60 / 90 / 120 min, or Set end… for an explicit end (can cross midnight).
  • Location — a venue/address. Turn on Notify me when I arrive for an on-the-spot reminder (needs "allow location all the time").
  • Meeting / join link — paste a Zoom/Teams/Meet link, or for a Google event tap Generate Google Meet link (added on the next sync).
  • Repeat — Once / Daily / Mon–Fri / Mon–Sat / Weekly / Monthly, or pick specific weekdays. Set an Ends date if you like.
  • Area — which part of life it belongs to (keeps your score accurate).
  • People — add participants from contacts; later, Call or WhatsApp them straight from the task. Numbers stay on your device.
  • Reminder — Notify me 15 min before it starts.
  • Notes and attachments — free text, a document link, or an image.

Open any task to see a live timer while it's running, its provenance ("Added in Saara / From Google · synced 8m ago"), and a full History (integrity ledger) — every step recorded, never rewritten.

4Run an event live — the agenda

An event isn't one checkbox — it's a sequence you perform. On an event, the Action items section lets you build a run-of-show:

  • Add task — give it a title, a start time, and a duration. Each new item pre-fills to the end of the last, so steps chain back-to-back (e.g. "2:00–2:15 · 15 min").
  • From notes — photograph your agenda notes and let AI turn each line into a timed item.
  • Start agenda — the hands-free runner: the current step auto-starts, a big timer counts down (turns red when you run over), your speaker notes show for that segment, and Up next previews the next. Tap Complete & next as you go.

At the end you get an Event report: completed X of Y, planned-vs-actual timing per item, and optional AI insights. Duplicate to another date clones the whole run-of-show, shifted to a new day.

5Keeping — and breaking — your word

Every released commitment ends in an honest disposition. From a task you'll see:

ActionMeaningScore
Mark completeYou kept it.Kept +1
RescheduleMove it and re-commit.Not counted either way
CancelBacking out of your own released word.Broken (counts against you)
No longer happeningAn external cancellation (event/invite dropped by the other side).Excluded — not counted
Missed (in the evening review)A no-show.Broken
Rejected / DeclinedYou said no, clearly.Excluded — not counted
Saara never marks a miss on its own. A commitment whose time has passed shows as "not answered" until you give it a disposition — usually in the evening review ("Complete your day"). Saying "no" is a complete, honest answer; only silence is the failure.

Marked something by mistake? Reopen puts it back — and that's recorded too. The ledger stays honest either way.

6Areas

Group commitments into the areas that make a whole life. Saara starts with Health, Exercise, Career, Finance, Relationships, Leisure. Tap Add area (up to 10) to add your own — name it, pick an icon and colour. Each area carries its own reliability, and the Progress wheel shows where you're strong and where you're thin.

7Progress & reliability

The Progress screen is your training log. Your score is simple and honest: kept ÷ committed.

  • Integrity across areas — a radar wheel; each area's fill = its score. Tap a wedge to drill in.
  • Reliability over time — Today / This week / This month / All-time, each with its % and your ladder level.
  • On time — a separate timeliness measure. A kept word that was late is still kept — timeliness is its own path, never a mark against the score.

The reliability ladder

LevelUp to
Beginner10%Your word isn't a habit yet.
Learner30%Building the muscle.
Amateur50%Consistency wavers.
Professional70%Dependable more often than not.
Champion85%Highly reliable.
Masterful100%Your word is your bond.
A path, not a scorecard. The number is a compass, not a verdict — integrity is trained, not achieved. The system owns the facts (did it happen, when); you own the judgment (how well); the people you gave your word to are the mirror.

8Sharing & reports

Share as a card (invites)

From any task/event, Share as card makes a beautiful branded card themed to its area. Pick Brand / Light / Dark, optionally Add a line with AI, then Share card (image + tappable details) or Share as text only — on WhatsApp, Gmail, anywhere. Only the title, time, place and links are shared — your notes and scores never leave.

Reports

  • Event report — Share report (text) or Export as file (a re-importable Markdown file).
  • Export all my data (Settings) — a .zip of every task, area and capture, wherever you choose to put it.

9Committed listeners

Integrity is relational — a promise isn't fully kept until it lands with the person you gave it to. Add a committed listener (with their consent), choose how reports reach them (WhatsApp / email / share sheet), the scope (all areas or one), and how often. Each period they rate how reliably you kept your word, and Saara compares it to your own score and suggests where to close the gap.

10Ask Saara & your AI key (bring your own)

The Saara tab understands plain language — "What did I commit with the coaches?" to find, or "Gym every Monday 6am" to create. It always shows a Confirm card before it acts.

Set up your key

Settings → Saara AI (your key). Your key is stored only in this device's secure keystore, and Saara talks to the provider directly — nothing goes through Realmaya.

  • Google Gemini — has a free tier. Tap Get a Google Gemini key, create a key, Paste it, then Test key.
  • Anthropic Claude — paid; same flow.
  • Pick a Model, or tap Check available models to list exactly what your key can use (this fixes "model not available" errors).
You're sharing conversation text with a third-party AI, billed to your own account — review their data settings. Saara only ever sends what you ask it to.

11Google Tasks & Calendar sync

Settings → Google Tasks sync. Two-way and automatic: creates, edits, completions and deletes flow both ways (last edit wins; deleting on one side deletes on the other). Saara tasks go to Google Tasks (they show in Google Calendar's Tasks layer); Saara events go to Google Calendar. Everything goes device → Google directly.

Connecting

  • Phone: tap Connect Google → choose your account → approve.
  • Desktop: paste a Google desktop client ID + Client secret (from Google Cloud Console → Credentials → OAuth client → Desktop app), then Connect Google (opens browser).
"Google hasn't verified this app"? While Saara's Google verification is in progress you may see this screen. It's expected — tap Advanced → Go to Saara (unsafe) → Allow. Nothing is wrong; the warning disappears once verification completes. Your data still goes only device → Google — never through us.

Day to day

  • Sync now — pull & push immediately.
  • Auto-sync — on open, and in the background (~30 min) while connected.
  • Check for duplicate copies — a one-time cleanup if an older version pushed repeat copies. It counts first and only removes Saara-created copies you confirm — items you didn't create in Saara are never touched.
  • Disconnect — stop syncing (your local data stays).

12Sync your devices — over Wi-Fi, no cloud

Keep phone and desktop in step directly, carrying what Google can't — your areas, history, provenance and people. Settings → Sync between my devices.

  • Sync over Wi-Fi — both on the same network: one device taps Show a code (a QR), the other Scan a code. They merge both ways in seconds. (A phone hotspot works if your Wi-Fi blocks device-to-device.)
  • Export / Import a ledger file — optionally password-protected. Share it over any app (WhatsApp, email, a folder) and import it on the other device.
  • Automatic folder sync — point both devices at a shared folder (OneDrive/Google Drive, detected automatically) protected by a passphrase; it syncs on open.

13How conflicts are handled

Saara is built so your devices can never truly "conflict":

  • Your history is never lost. The integrity ledger is append-only — a mistake is answered with a correcting entry, never a rewrite. Merging two devices can only add facts, so your commitment history and score are always complete.
  • For the current details of a task (title, time, area), the most recent edit wins (by timestamp). If you edit the same field on two devices before they sync, the newer edit is kept.
  • Google sync is two-way with the same rule — last edit wins; deleting on one side deletes on the other.

Staying in sync

The sync chip in the top bar shows amber Sync ("changes not yet synced — tap to sync") or green In sync. It matters most on Progress — a device that hasn't synced shows only a partial score, so sync before you judge the number.

Schedule conflicts

If two things overlap, Today shows a gentle banner ("N overlaps ahead — tap to see them, keep or change, your call"), and the task form warns when you're double-booking. It's always advisory — Saara never blocks you from committing.

15Privacy & your data

  • On-device & encrypted. Your ledger lives in an encrypted database on your device. No account, no server, no telemetry.
  • Your AI, your key. AI calls go device → provider directly; the key is in your OS keystore.
  • Google sync goes device → Google directly — never through Realmaya.
  • Yours to take. Export a full .zip or an encrypted ledger anytime; the ledger is its own backup.

16Use-case recipes

Capture

A screenshot into an event

Turn a Zoom/Meet invite you were sent into a real calendar event.

  1. + Task → From a screenshot / photo
  2. Pick the invite image → choose Event
  3. AI fills the title, time and join link — check it against the image
  4. Release → it's on your Google Calendar
Perform

Run a 2-hour training live

Break a big event into a timed run-of-show you execute on stage.

  1. Create the event (2h) → open it
  2. Action items → add each segment with a start & duration (they chain automatically)
  3. On the day: Start agenda → tick Complete & next as you go
  4. Finish → review the Event report
Sync

Phone ↔ laptop, no cloud

Move your whole record between your own devices in seconds.

  1. Both on the same Wi-Fi
  2. Settings → Sync between my devices → Sync over Wi-Fi
  3. Laptop: Show a code · Phone: Scan a code
  4. They match — history, areas and all
Measure

A money goal, scored

Money is just a measurable — quantify it and it's real.

  1. Add a task like "Save ₹5,000 this month" in the Finance area
  2. Attach the number as a measurable result
  3. Hit the number → kept (+1); fall short → not kept. No grey zone.
Resolve

Untangle a clash

Two commitments overlap — decide honestly.

  1. Today shows "N overlaps ahead" → tap it
  2. See the clashing items side by side
  3. Keep one and Reschedule the other — cleanly, on the record
Invite

Invite someone on WhatsApp

Send a commitment as a beautiful card.

  1. Open the event → Share as card
  2. Pick Brand, optionally add an AI line
  3. Share card → WhatsApp — the join link stays tappable
