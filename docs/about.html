About

Give your word. Keep it.

Saara is a private, local-first companion for the commitments that make up your life — a place to give your word, keep it, and watch your reliability grow across every space you live in.

What Saara is

Most tools help you make a list. Saara helps you keep it. It treats every commitment as something you gave — to yourself, your family, your team, or the wider world — and turns keeping it into a practice you can measure and improve. Part personal-integrity training, part productivity companion.

The idea: P-Integrity

Integrity isn't a personality trait — it's a path. In Saara your kept word radiates outward through four widening spaces — Personal, Private, Peers and Public — and your reliability climbs a ladder from Learner to Masterful as you go. The point isn't a perfect scorecard; it's becoming whole and complete across every space of your life.

Private by design

  • ✓No account, no cloud server. Saara runs on your own devices.
  • ✓Encrypted on device. Your ledger lives in an encrypted database that never leaves your machines.
  • ✓No ads, no tracking. You are not the product.
  • ✓Serverless sync. Devices reconcile over your own Wi-Fi — the ledger is its own backup.
